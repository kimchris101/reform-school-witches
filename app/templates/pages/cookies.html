Cookie Policy

Effective Date: August 2026

1. How We Use Cookies

The Reform School for Witches ("RSFW") uses essential HTTP cookies to deliver core interactive visual novel functionality. Because our portal runs on a zero-database architecture, cookies are required to store your game progress and session authorization locally on your device.

2. Essential Cookies We Set

rsfw_member_token

Authenticates that you have completed the Intake Exam and grants access to the interactive Script Engine.

sanctity

Stores your current Sanctity alignment percentage (0–100%) based on your choices in the story engine.

corruption

Stores your current Corruption alignment percentage (0–100%) based on your choices in the story engine.

3. Third-Party & Advertising Cookies

We do not use third-party tracking pixels, advertising cookies, or cross-site telemetry services. All cookies set by this domain are strictly first-party and essential for gameplay.

4. Managing Your Cookies

You can clear or block cookies at any time via your browser settings. Please note that clearing cookies will reset your active story choices, Sanctity/Corruption metrics, and Diagnostic clearance.
