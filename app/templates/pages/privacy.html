Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 2026

1. Data Storage & Supabase Architecture

The Reform School for Witches ("RSFW", "we", "our") respects visitor privacy. We utilize Supabase—a cloud-hosted PostgreSQL database platform—for secure data storage, account authentication, and diagnostic intake exam processing. Any data submitted during intake registration or member authentication is encrypted in transit and stored securely within our private Supabase database infrastructure.

2. Local Browser Cookies & Game State

In addition to server-side database storage, we utilize essential client-side HTTP cookies to maintain real-time interactive game states in your browser without requiring constant database roundtrips. Essential cookies set by this domain include:

  • rsfw_member_token: Validates intake clearance and authorization status.
  • sanctity: Tracks progress metric delta (0–100%) during interactive choices.
  • corruption: Tracks progress metric delta (0–100%) during interactive choices.

3. Local Manuscript Queries

Search queries submitted via the Archival Index are processed locally on our application server against indexed manuscript files. Search queries are not logged for advertising purposes, nor are they transmitted to third-party artificial intelligence platforms.

4. Third-Party Sharing & Telemetry

We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal information or diagnostic exam responses. Information stored in our Supabase database is strictly used to deliver core application functionality, access control, and user session management.

5. Contact & Inquiries

For questions regarding our privacy directives, data deletion requests, or archival compliance, contact our founders at info@redcandledigital.io.com.
