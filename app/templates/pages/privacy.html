Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 2026

1. Overview & Zero-Database Architecture

The Reform School for Witches ("RSFW", "we", "our") respects visitor privacy. Our interactive script engine operates on a zero-database, stateless architecture. We do not maintain server-side user accounts, tracking profiles, or personal identity databases.

2. Data Collection & Cookies

We do not collect personal identifying information (PII) without explicit intake registration. To maintain game state across session choices, we utilize essential client-side HTTP cookies:

  • rsfw_member_token: Validates intake exam authorization.
  • sanctity: Tracks progress metric delta (0-100%).
  • corruption: Tracks progress metric delta (0-100%).

3. Local PDF & Manuscript Queries

Queries submitted via the Archival Index are processed locally on the server against indexed manuscript files. Queries are not transmitted to third-party artificial intelligence services or external advertisers.

4. Contact & Inquiries

For questions regarding our privacy directives or archival compliance, contact the Academy Registrar at admin@reformschoolforwitches.com.
