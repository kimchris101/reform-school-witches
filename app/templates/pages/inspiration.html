Spiritual Inspiration

The real-world martyrs and mystics who inspired the universe of The Reform School for Witches.

Behind the shadowy corridors of Our Lady of Tears Academy and the dark fictional tapestry of The Reform School for Witches lies a profound spiritual foundation. The creative universe was built directly upon the lives, sacrifices, and extraordinary witness of two women: Blessed Sister Maria Laura Mainetti and Sister Amalia of Jesus Flagellated.

Their unflinching faith in the face of demonic hatred, darkness, and martyrdom serves as the core pillar for our themes of sacramental realism, redemption, and spiritual warfare.

Martyr of Charity (1939–2000)

Blessed Sister Maria Laura Mainetti

An Italian Sister of the Cross who devoted her life to vulnerable youth in Chiavenna. On June 6, 2000, she was lured into a trap by three teenage girls participating in a ritual murder. As she was being attacked, her final words were not of fear or hatred, but a prayer of forgiveness for her killers: "Lord, forgive them."

✝ Beatified as a Martyr in odium fidei (in hatred of the faith) by the Catholic Church.
Mystic of the Tears (1901–1977)

Sister Amalia of Jesus Flagellated

A Brazilian nun of the Missionaries of the Crucified Jesus who received extraordinary mystical revelations regarding Our Lady of Tears. Through her visions, the devotion of the Crown of Tears was revealed—a powerful spiritual weapon against evil where the tears of the Blessed Mother bind the forces of darkness.

☩ Key inspiration for Our Lady of Tears Academy and the sacramental relics within the lore.

"The light shines in the darkness, and the darkness has not overcome it."

Dedicated in reverence to the memory, sanctity, and eternal intercession of Sister Maria Laura and Sister Amalia.
