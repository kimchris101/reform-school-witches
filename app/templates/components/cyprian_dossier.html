[ ARCHDIOCESAN NOTE — FATHER MANUEL ]

"Do not judge the boy by the filth that fed him. The Sanguine Knot was tied by his father, not his infant will. The Lord does not destroy a vessel simply because it was used by a thief. When the sorcerer’s books burn, the ashes leave a mark. If he wakes... he will not be a Siphon. He will be a blade."
